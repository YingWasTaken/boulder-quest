Safety and terms of use

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Climbing carries risk

Boulder Quest suggests physical challenges to play at climbing gyms. Climbing can cause injury. By playing you accept that:

  • Each player decides whether to take on a challenge and does so at their own risk.
  • You must always follow the gym's rules and staff instructions, which come before any challenge.
  • Warm up before playing and don't attempt challenges beyond your level or fitness. You can change or skip any card.
  • Challenges with eyes closed, jumps, extra weight or dynamic moves should only be done on low, easy boulders, over mats, with a clear fall zone.
  • Never climb above another person or put anyone at risk.
  • Children must play under adult supervision.

2. Respect other people

Some challenges involve other people. Always ask before involving someone, don't bother anyone who doesn't want to take part, and never take photos or videos of anyone without their consent.
