Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what data Boulder Quest (the Android app and the web version) handles and how. In short: Boulder Quest does not collect or send any personal data. Everything you type stays on your device.

1. Controller

Controller: Pablo Casamián. Privacy contact: pablocasamianestudios@gmail.com.

2. What is stored and where

  • Player names and colours you type when you set up a game.
  • Game state: points, turn, cards and settings (language, sound, rounds, difficulty).

This data is kept in your device's local storage so you can resume a game. It is never sent to a server, is not linked to any account and nobody but you can access it. We suggest using nicknames rather than full names.

3. What we don't do

  • No sign-up and no user accounts.
  • No analytics, advertising, trackers or third-party cookies.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or device identifiers.
  • We never sell or share data with third parties.

4. App permissions

The app only uses vibration to give feedback when you complete or fail a challenge. It asks for no other permissions.

5. Sharing results

When you tap “Share Victory”, the app creates an image of the scoreboard on your device and opens the system share sheet. You choose which app to share it with and with whom; that app's own policy then applies.

6. External links

The app links to our Discord community. Opening it takes you out of Boulder Quest, and Discord's privacy policy applies.

7. Web version

The web version (boulder-quest.vercel.app) is hosted by Vercel Inc. (United States), which, like any server, logs technical connection data (IP address, date, browser) to serve the page and for security, for a limited time. Vercel acts as a data processor and provides safeguards for international transfers (EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses); its policy is at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy. The Android app does not connect to Vercel. Fonts are served from our own server, with no connections to third-party services.

8. Children

Boulder Quest collects no data from anyone, children included. Children under 14 should play under adult supervision, as climbing gyms also require.

9. Your rights

Because the data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by removing the players, clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling it. For any question, write to pablocasamianestudios@gmail.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD at aepd.es).

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here with its date.
